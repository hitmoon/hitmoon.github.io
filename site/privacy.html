1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the LifePage iOS app, its widgets and its extensions (together, “LifePage”), and to these legal and support pages. LifePage is published by the LifePage developer (“we”, “us” or “our”). It does not apply to Apple’s App Store, iCloud or other Apple services, which are governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

We do not operate a server that receives your personal data. The app is designed to work offline, and there is no account system.

This privacy policy and the support pages are static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, such as your IP address, to deliver them under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no cookies, analytics or tracking to these pages.

2. Data stored on your device

LifePage stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Your birthday and the precision you choose (day, month or year).
  • The lifespan assumption you choose and related display preferences.
  • Theme, palette, grid style, color-blind patterns, tone, selected lens and display unit.
  • Reminder preferences and the information needed to schedule local notifications.
  • The local Pro entitlement cache used to unlock purchased features offline.
  • A small widget snapshot shared with the LifePage widget through Apple’s App Group.

This information stays on your device unless you explicitly enable iCloud settings sync, as described below. It is not sent to us, and we cannot read it.

3. Optional iCloud settings sync

iCloud settings sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, the app stores a small envelope in your private iCloud account using Apple’s key-value storage. The envelope contains only:

  • Theme, palette, grid style and color-blind pattern.
  • Tone, selected lens and display unit.
  • Reminder preferences.
  • Life-stage boundaries.

It never contains your birthday, your lifespan assumption, your Pro entitlement or the iCloud toggle itself. Apple processes the data on your behalf; we cannot access your private iCloud account. If iCloud is unavailable or you are offline, LifePage keeps using local settings.

Turning sync off deletes the cloud copy. Deleting all data in the app removes the profile and settings stored on the device; to remove an iCloud settings copy, turn off iCloud settings sync as well.

4. In-app purchases

LifePage Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card, billing address or Apple Account details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the payment and purchase records.

The app stores a local entitlement so Pro features work offline. You can restore a purchase at any time with “Restore purchases” in the Pro sheet. Apple may provide us with aggregate sales and trends reports in App Store Connect; those reports do not identify you personally.

5. Notifications

Birthday, New Year and quarterly reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are off by default, and the app asks for notification permission only when you enable a reminder. There is no push server and no notification content is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

6. Photos

When you save a poster or annual report, LifePage requests add-only access to your photo library. The app writes the image you created and does not read, browse or upload your photo library.

7. Widgets and App Group

The Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets read a small snapshot from an App Group shared between the app and its widget extension. This snapshot stays on your device and is not sent to us. Widgets do not use a network connection.

8. Analytics, ads and tracking

LifePage contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell, rent or share personal information. The app does not request AppTrackingTransparency permission because it does not track you.

Apple may provide aggregate App Store analytics to developers. That data is controlled by Apple and is not linked to your identity by us.

9. Export and deletion

You can export your data as a JSON file from Settings at any time. Deleting all data removes your profile, settings and local preferences from the device and returns the app to onboarding. It does not cancel or refund a Pro purchase, and it does not delete Apple’s purchase records; you can restore a purchase later through the App Store.

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Apple may retain purchase records and iCloud copies according to Apple’s policies. To remove an iCloud settings copy, turn off iCloud settings sync before uninstalling or from another signed-in device.

10. Children and age rating

LifePage is rated 4+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no account and does not send personal data to us, there is no developer-side child profile to delete.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data. LifePage keeps the relevant data on your device and does not transmit it to us, so you can exercise most of these choices directly in the app. For iCloud data, use Apple’s iCloud settings and privacy tools. For purchase records, use Apple’s App Store account tools.

We do not sell personal information. If you have a privacy request, contact us using the details below and we will explain what we can do. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and use them only to respond and keep minimal support records for as long as needed to handle the request. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. If a change is material, we will provide a clear notice in the app or on this page.

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 LifePage（人生一页）iOS 应用、小组件及其扩展（合称 “LifePage”），以及这些法务和支持页面。LifePage 由 LifePage 开发者 （“我们”）发布。本政策不适用于 Apple 的 App Store、iCloud 或其他 Apple 服务；这些服务受 Apple 自己的条款和隐私政策约束。

我们不运营接收你个人数据的服务器。应用按离线优先设计，没有账号系统。

本隐私政策和支持页面是由 GitHub Pages 托管的静态页面。GitHub 可能根据 GitHub 隐私声明 处理 IP 地址等技术请求数据以提供页面。我们不会在这些页面中添加 Cookie、 分析或追踪。

2. 保存在本机的数据

LifePage 会在你的设备上保存以下信息：

  • 你的生日，以及你选择的精度（日、月或年）。
  • 你选择的寿命假设和相关显示偏好。
  • 主题、配色、网格样式、色盲图案、语气、当前镜头和显示单位。
  • 提醒偏好，以及安排本地通知所需的信息。
  • 用于离线解锁已购功能的本地 Pro 权益缓存。
  • 通过 Apple App Group 与 LifePage 小组件共享的少量快照。

除非你主动开启下述 iCloud 设置同步，这些信息只留在本机。它们不会发送 给我们，我们也无法读取。

3. 可选的 iCloud 设置同步

iCloud 设置同步默认关闭。如果你在设置中开启，应用会通过 Apple 的键值存储，在你私人的 iCloud 账户中保存一个小信封。信封只包含：

  • 主题、配色、网格样式和色盲图案。
  • 语气、当前镜头和显示单位。
  • 提醒偏好。
  • 人生阶段边界。

它绝不包含你的生日、寿命假设、Pro 权益或 iCloud 开关本身。Apple 代表你 处理这些数据；我们无法访问你的私人 iCloud。iCloud 不可用或离线时， LifePage 会继续使用本地设置。

关闭同步会删除云端副本。删除应用中的全部数据会移除设备上的资料和设置； 如果要同时删除 iCloud 设置副本，请一并关闭 iCloud 设置同步。

4. 应用内购买

LifePage Pro 是一次性、非消耗型应用内购买，由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。 我们不会收到或保存你的银行卡、账单地址或 Apple 账户信息。支付和购买记录 适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

应用会保存本地权益，以便离线使用 Pro 功能。你可以随时在 Pro 页面使用 “恢复购买”。Apple 可能通过 App Store Connect 向我们提供汇总销售和趋势 报告；这些报告不会识别你的个人身份。

5. 通知

生日、新年和季度回顾提醒是本机安排的通知，默认关闭。只有当你开启提醒时， 应用才会请求通知权限。没有推送服务器，通知内容不会发送给我们。你可以随时 在应用内或 iOS 设置中关闭提醒。

6. 相册

保存海报或年度报告时，LifePage 会请求相册的“仅添加”权限。应用只写入你 创建的图片，不会读取、浏览或上传你的相册。

7. 小组件与 App Group

主屏幕、锁屏和 StandBy 小组件会读取应用与小组件扩展共享的 App Group 快照。快照保存在你的设备上，不会发送给我们。小组件不联网。

8. 分析、广告与追踪

LifePage 不包含第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK。我们不会跨应用或网站追踪你， 也不会出售、出租或共享个人信息。应用不请求 AppTrackingTransparency 权限，因为它不追踪你。

Apple 可能向开发者提供汇总的 App Store 分析数据。这些数据由 Apple 控制， 我们不会将其与你的身份关联。

9. 导出与删除

你可以随时在设置中以 JSON 导出数据。删除全部数据会移除设备上的资料、 设置和本地偏好，并回到引导页。它不会取消或退款 Pro 购买，也不会删除 Apple 的购买记录；之后仍可通过 App Store 恢复购买。

卸载应用会移除其本地数据。Apple 可能根据其政策保留购买记录和 iCloud 副本。如需删除 iCloud 设置副本，请在卸载前或从另一台已登录设备关闭 iCloud 设置同步。

10. 儿童与年龄分级

LifePage 的年龄分级为 4+，并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童 的个人信息。由于应用没有账号，也不会向我们发送个人数据，因此不存在需要 删除的开发者侧儿童档案。

11. 你的隐私权利

根据你所在的地区，你可能拥有访问、更正、导出或删除个人数据的权利。 LifePage 将相关数据保存在你的设备上，不会传输给我们，因此大多数选择可以 直接在应用内完成。iCloud 数据请使用 Apple 的 iCloud 设置和隐私工具； 购买记录请使用 Apple 的 App Store 账户工具。

我们不出售个人信息。如果你有隐私请求，请通过下方联系方式联系我们， 我们会说明可以协助的范围。如果你给我们发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和 邮件内容，并仅用于回复和保留最少量的支持记录，且只在处理请求所需的期限内 保留。如果你位于欧洲经济区或 英国，你也可以向当地数据保护机构投诉。

12. 政策更新

当应用发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。更新版本会发布在本页面，并标注新的 “最后更新”日期。如果变更重大，我们会在应用内或本页面提供明确提示。
