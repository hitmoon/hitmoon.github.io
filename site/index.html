Data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

Your birthday, lifespan assumption, display preferences, reminders and local Pro cache stay on your device. LifePage has no account and no developer server. If you turn on iCloud settings sync, only selected preferences are stored in your private iCloud account; your birthday, lifespan, Pro entitlement and the sync toggle are never included. Read the Privacy Policy for the full list.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open Settings → Data & privacy. You can export a JSON copy of your data or delete all data stored by the app. Deleting all data returns the app to onboarding. It does not cancel or refund Pro, and it does not delete Apple’s purchase records.

To remove an iCloud settings copy, also turn off iCloud settings sync. To remove local data entirely, uninstall the app.

Do you collect analytics or show ads?

No. LifePage contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not ask for AppTrackingTransparency permission. Apple may provide us with aggregate App Store reports, but those do not identify you personally.

Why is my birthday not synced through iCloud?

That is intentional. iCloud settings sync is designed to move preferences only, not the facts about your life. Your birthday and lifespan assumption stay on each device. Sync is off by default.

Pro and purchases

What is included in LifePage Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks:

  • All advanced lenses
  • What-if lifespan preview
  • Watermark-free posters
  • Annual report
  • Custom life-stage boundaries

The exact price and currency are shown by the App Store before you confirm the purchase.

Does Pro renew automatically?

No. Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It is not a subscription, there is no automatic renewal and there is no recurring charge. If Family Sharing is enabled for the product, eligible family members may be able to use the same purchase.

How do I restore a purchase?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase, then open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. You can reach the Pro screen from Settings or from a locked lens. No purchase code is needed.

I paid, but Pro is still locked. What should I do?

Check your connection, confirm you are using the purchasing Apple Account and tap Restore purchases again. If the App Store does not show the purchase, check your purchase history or contact Apple Support. You can also email us with the purchase date and product name; never send card numbers or Apple Account passwords.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes all payments and refunds. Use reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support. We cannot issue a refund directly, and deleting the app does not cancel a purchase.

App, widgets and reminders

My widget is not updating.

Open LifePage once after changing your birthday, lifespan or appearance so the widget snapshot can refresh. iOS decides when widgets update, so a change may not appear immediately. Try removing and re-adding the widget, and check whether Low Power Mode is delaying refreshes. Widgets do not use a network connection.

My reminders are not arriving.

Reminders are off by default. Enable the reminder you want in LifePage settings, allow notifications when iOS asks, and check Settings → Notifications → LifePage. A Focus mode or a deleted schedule can also suppress a reminder.

iCloud settings sync is not working.

Confirm that you are signed in to iCloud, that both devices use the same Apple Account and that iCloud settings sync is on. Turn the toggle off and on again, then open the app on both devices. Only preferences sync; your birthday and lifespan are intentionally excluded.

Is LifePage medical, financial or life-expectancy advice?

No. LifePage is a reflection tool. The lifespan is an assumption you choose, not a prediction. The app does not provide medical, financial, legal or life-expectancy advice. Consult a qualified professional for those questions.

数据与隐私

我的数据保存在哪里？

你的生日、寿命假设、显示偏好、提醒和本地 Pro 缓存都保存在你的设备上。 LifePage 没有账号，也没有开发者服务器。如果你开启 iCloud 设置同步， 只有部分偏好会保存在你私人的 iCloud 账户中；生日、寿命假设、Pro 权益和同步开关绝不会包含在内。完整清单请见 隐私政策。

如何导出或删除我的数据？

打开“设置”→“数据与隐私”。你可以导出 JSON 数据副本，或删除应用保存的全部 数据。删除全部数据会让应用回到引导页；它不会取消或退款 Pro，也不会 删除 Apple 的购买记录。

如需删除 iCloud 设置副本，请同时关闭 iCloud 设置同步。如需彻底移除 本地数据，请卸载应用。

你们会收集分析数据或展示广告吗？

不会。LifePage 不包含第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK，也不会请求 AppTrackingTransparency 权限。Apple 可能向我们提供汇总的 App Store 报告，但这些报告不会识别你的个人身份。

为什么我的生日没有通过 iCloud 同步？

这是有意设计。iCloud 设置同步只移动偏好，不移动关于你人生的事实。 生日和寿命假设留在各自的设备上。同步默认关闭。

Pro 与购买

LifePage Pro 包含什么？

Pro 是一次性购买，解锁：

  • 全部高级镜头
  • What-if 寿命预览
  • 无水印海报
  • 年度报告
  • 自定义人生阶段边界

具体价格和币种会在你确认购买前由 App Store 显示。

Pro 会自动续费吗？

不会。Pro 是一次性、非消耗型应用内购买，不是订阅，不会自动续费， 也没有周期性扣款。如果商品开启了家人共享，符合条件的家庭成员可能 可以使用同一份购买。

如何恢复购买？

确认你登录的是购买时使用的同一个 Apple 账户，然后打开 Pro 页面， 点击恢复购买。你可以从“设置”或某个锁定镜头进入 Pro 页面，不需要购买码。

我付款了，但 Pro 仍是锁定状态，怎么办？

请检查网络，确认使用的是购买时的 Apple 账户，然后再次点击 恢复购买。如果 App Store 仍不显示购买，请查看购买 历史或联系 Apple 支持。你也可以把购买日期和商品名称发给我们； 请不要发送银行卡号或 Apple 账户密码。

如何申请退款？

Apple 处理所有支付和退款。请使用 reportaproblem.apple.com 或联系 Apple 支持。我们无法直接退款，删除应用也不会取消购买。

应用、小组件与提醒

小组件没有更新。

修改生日、寿命或外观后，请打开一次 LifePage，让小组件快照刷新。 iOS 决定小组件的刷新时机，所以变化可能不会立刻出现。可以尝试移除并 重新添加小组件，并检查低电量模式是否延迟了刷新。小组件不联网。

提醒没有送达。

提醒默认关闭。请在 LifePage 设置中开启需要的提醒，在 iOS 询问时允许 通知，并检查“设置”→“通知”→“LifePage”。专注模式或已删除的提醒计划 也可能阻止提醒送达。

iCloud 设置同步不工作。

请确认已登录 iCloud、两台设备使用同一个 Apple 账户，并且 iCloud 设置同步已开启。关闭再开启开关，然后在两台设备上分别打开应用。 只同步偏好；生日和寿命假设有意不同步。

LifePage 是医疗、财务或寿命预测建议吗？

不是。LifePage 是一面看时间的镜子。寿命是你选择的假设，不是预测。 本应用不提供医疗、财务、法律或寿命预测建议；这些问题请咨询合格的 专业人士。
